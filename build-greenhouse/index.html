Building a greenhouse

We built a 20 x 32 greenhouse. It was a lot of work but well worth the effort.

I bought a “kit” that had everything except the doors for each end section. I had to drill “a lot” of holes in that pipe. But the instructions that came with the kit were pretty good. I later bought the shade cloth to block the harsh summer sun and keep it cooler inside.
