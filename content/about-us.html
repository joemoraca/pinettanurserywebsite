Joe & Rhonda Moraca are living the dream on their third rural property. In between there were several stretches of city life in Sarasota and Gainesville. Before we bought our current property we traveled full time in our RV for about three years. We hit 45 of 50 states in the USA and plan on continuing to travel part-time.

We both love rural living and growing plants. Our morning ritual includes a walk around the property picking berries and whatever else we find that is ready to eat.
